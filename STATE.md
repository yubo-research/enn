## Operator ruling on design method (2026-11-15)
- The cliff at the cap is not a property of the problem. It comes from treating memory as the store: letting the page cache hold everything until it is full is not an algorithm, and shows no understanding of how the data is used. Stop treating "a step of cold-read size is paid by any disk design" (0224, 0225, 0231) as settled; that conclusion rests on a RAM-until-full design.
- Design order: (1) the best on-disk algorithm you can build, O(lnN) and fast, judged by its true on-disk cost from N=1 (below the cap, today's "disk reads" are really cache hits; 0225: 1.7 vs ~200 us). (2) Then use memory as a cache, choosing what to keep from measured access patterns: what every operation touches, and what the workload touches often. A cache chosen this way degrades gradually as N grows (contributing to the lnN slope), not all at once at the cap.
- Quality target is statistical parity with main (error bars, tests), not exactness; approximations are allowed.
- Beating main at small N is an engineering goal, not a conflict with the above.

## Operator ruling on priorities (2026-11-15, after 0240)
- Priority: a+b lnN scaling of both add and query, from N=1 through data >=10x the cap, comes before beating main on in-RAM wall time. Do not reject a change for being slower than main in RAM if it is what makes the curves lnN; record the in-RAM cost and move on. (Answers the add-parity question from 0226 and the slower-add question from 0238.)
- A constant-factor increase in query cost is acceptable in exchange for lnN adds, provided queries stay a+b lnN and quality stays at statistical parity. (Answers the 0240 question; size the constant by measurement, don't wait for a number from the operator.)
- 0240 does not refute per-level buffering: it used 64-row buffers, while ideas.md P1 and 0200 say buffers must be much larger than a page (~1000 rows). Test buffering at the size the theory requires (buffer tree, Arge 2003; Be-tree, Bender et al. 2015) before concluding anything.
- Queries are already a+b lnN in true on-disk cost (0235-0238). The open work is (a) lnN adds across the cap and (b) step 2 of the design method: a cache chosen from measured access patterns.

## Operator ruling on evaluation (2026-11-16, after 0250)
